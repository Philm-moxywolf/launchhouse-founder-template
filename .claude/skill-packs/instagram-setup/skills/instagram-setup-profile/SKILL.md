# Instagram profile copy

1. Read `.claude/skill-packs/instagram-setup/pack.md` and `.claude/skill-packs/instagram-setup/references/knowledge.md` first.
2. Confirm Track is `b2c` in the Founder Brain. If not, stop (see `instagram-setup-expert`, step 2).
3. Read `brain/founder-brain.md`'s Offer, Audience, Proof, and Voice sections, and `brain/voice-samples/` if any files exist there.
4. Draft, in the founder's own words and voice, never a generic template:
   - **Bio.** At most 150 characters (Instagram's own limit). What the founder does, who it is for, and the one line that makes someone want to read on.
   - **Link in bio.** Point at the founder's GoHighLevel booking link or lead form if one exists in the Brain's Channels section; a plain, unbranded URL only if nothing better exists yet, and say so.
   - **Highlights plan.** Two or three highlight names that would serve this audience (for example, the offer, proof, or how-it-works), named plainly. Never suggest cover-image styling; that is the founder's own design choice.
5. Never state a number, result, or claim the Brain has not confirmed under its Proof section (rule 5). If the founder's own notes mention a figure not yet confirmed, ask them to confirm it and add it to the Brain first, rather than writing it in.
6. Route the full draft through the `rules-reviewer` agent before showing it to the founder.
7. Show the draft and stop. The founder copies it into Instagram themselves; this skill never pastes it in, since Claude never signs in to a founder's Instagram (`../../../references/social-accounts.md`).
